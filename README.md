# CogniTwin – AI-Powered Cognitive Digital Twin for MSMEs

CogniTwin is an AI-powered business dashboard designed to help MSMEs and small businesses manage, analyze, and understand their business data efficiently.

🔗 **Live Website:**  
https://project-orcin-beta-71.vercel.app

🔗 **GitHub Repository:**

## 🚀 Features

- 📊 Interactive Business Dashboard
- 🧾 Invoice Processing and Management
- 📁 CSV Dataset Upload
- 💰 Sales and Revenue Analysis
- 📈 Business Performance Visualization
- 🤖 AI-powered Business Insights
- 🔍 Data Analysis and Trend Detection
- 📋 Invoice Status Tracking
- 📅 Payment and Due-Date Monitoring
- 📌 Easy-to-use Interface

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective of CogniTwin is to provide small and medium-sized businesses with a simple digital platform that converts business data into meaningful insights.

It helps business owners understand their business performance, monitor invoices and payments, and make better data-driven decisions.

## 📊 Invoice Processing

CogniTwin supports invoice-related data such as:

- Invoice ID
- Invoice Date
- Due Date
- Vendor Name
- Customer Name
- Item Description
- Quantity
- Subtotal
- GST
- Total Amount
- Payment Status
- Processing Status
- Payment Method

## 📁 Dataset

The project includes a synthetic invoice-processing dataset containing sample business transactions.

The dataset can be uploaded to the dashboard for analysis and visualization.

> **Note:** The dataset is synthetic and does not contain real customer or business information.
